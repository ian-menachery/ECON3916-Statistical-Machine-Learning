The Architecture of Dimensionality: Hedonic Pricing & the FWL Theorem

Objective
To empirically demonstrate the mathematical mechanics of ceteris paribus in multivariate algorithms by estimating a hedonic pricing model for the California real estate market and manually executing the Frisch-Waugh-Lovell (FWL) theorem to neutralize omitted variable bias.

Methodology
- Data Pipeline & Baseline Estimation: Ingested 2026 California real estate metrics (focusing on Sale_Price, Property_Age, and Distance_to_Tech_Hub) and estimated a naive bivariate Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression to observe the initial, biased coefficient of physical depreciation.
- Multivariate Expansion: Constructed a multivariate OLS model to control for geographical proximity to major technology hubs, observing the magnitude and directional shifts in the age coefficient once the spatial confounder was absorbed by the multidimensional hyperplane.
- Variance Isolation: Manually executed the FWL theorem by partialling out the variance of the confounding spatial variable (Distance_to_Tech_Hub) from both the target variable (Sale_Price) and the feature of interest (Property_Age). 
- Residual Extraction & Proof: Extracted the residuals from both intermediary models and regressed the isolated price residuals strictly against the isolated age residuals to mathematically capture the pure, uncorrelated signal.

Key Findings
The baseline bivariate model exhibited severe Omitted Variable Bias (OVB), falsely attributing mathematical credit to Property_Age due to its underlying collinearity with lucrative coastal tech hubs. By manually executing the FWL theorem, the shared covariance between physical aging and geographic location was systematically stripped away. The resulting residual-on-residual regression yielded an exact mathematical match to the coefficient generated by the multivariate algorithm. This serves as an epistemological proof of how high-dimensional OLS networks successfully isolate pure signals and enforce ceteris paribus conditions to prevent catastrophic mispricing scenarios.
